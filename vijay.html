vijay

Joseph Vijay Chandrasekhar, known mononymously as Vijay, is a renowned Indian actor, dancer, and philanthropist who has dominated Tamil cinema for decades. Popularly called "Thalapathy" (Commander), he has acted in over 60 films, often playing action-oriented roles. In 2024, he entered politics by launching his party, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK)
